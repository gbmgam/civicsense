CivicGuide is an AI-powered government service assistant designed to help citizens find the right government department and exact office for their needs.

Users simply describe what they need, such as “I need a DDO signature” or “I want to apply for a birth certificate.” CivicGuide asks relevant follow-up questions, understands their location, and guides them step by step.

Key Features
🔎 AI-powered service search
💬 Interactive questions based on the user’s task
📍 Location-based guidance — District, Taluk, Area, etc.
🏢 Finds the correct government department and office
🗂️ Shows required documents and procedures
🏛️ Provides building, block, floor, counter, and desk details
🗺️ Google Maps and directions
🖼️ Office image when reliable information is available
📞 Office timings and contact details
📱 Simple, accessible, mobile-friendly interface
Core Flow

User Task → AI Questions → Location → Department → Office → Documents → Exact Counter/Desk → Map & Directions

Goal: Make government services easier by helping citizens reach the right place, for the right service, with the right documents.
